## Key Insights

**1. The Funnel Mental Model**
Upwork is a pipe. Track every conversion point. Fix blockages as high up the funnel as possible — a 1% improvement in views-to-clicks impacts every downstream number more than a 1% improvement in close rate.

**2. Ryan Does Not Send Proposals**
He is fully inbound from profile boosting. This is the endgame state — not the starting state. Emmanuel is at the proposal stage. Ryan's boosting model becomes relevant once profile has JSS, reviews, and algorithm ranking.

**3. Tracking is Prerequisite to Scaling**
Without these numbers, you don't know which part of your funnel is broken. Most people Ryan coaches never track their northstar numbers. That's why they can't scale.

**4. The Free Sheet**
Ryan gives away the exact Google Sheet he uses with 1:1 students. All formulas are automated. Go to the video description to get it.

**5. A TOS-Compliant Upwork Calendar is Coming**
Ryan is building a Upwork-native calendar tool and releasing it free on YouTube. Upwork currently has no native call booking tracker.

**6. Close Rate Signal**
If close rate >40%, raise prices. You're leaving money on the table. A 30% close at $10K is better than 40% close at $7K.
