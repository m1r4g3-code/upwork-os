## Strategy Notes

**Archetype:** Cautious small business owner. Budget-conscious. Has probably been burned by a freelancer or agency before. Runs the business herself or with minimal help.

**Real fear:** Paying for something and seeing no change. Not "bad design" — wasted money.

**What she needs to believe:** The site is losing her real money right now, and one extra sale from a fixed homepage pays for the entire job. The math has to feel obvious to her, not promised.

**Opening observation used:** Sold-out homepage — the most visceral, specific, instantly verifiable problem. She can open her own site right now and see it.

**Proof point:** Shopify redesign, 1 week, orders within first two weeks. Needs a proper noun if Emmanuel has one — fill in actual client name or project name before sending.

**Closing question:** "Do you know roughly how many people visit the site each month?" — Low friction, no commitment, makes her calculate the scale of the loss herself.

**Automation add-on pitch:** Held back from the Loom. If she replies, introduce it in the follow-up conversation as a separate tier. Do not bundle it into the first ask.

## Site Audit Summary (from WebFetch — 2026-07-10)

**Business:** Petit Lit Furniture — children's beds, bunk beds, trundle beds, mattresses. Brooklyn, NY. Shopify store.
**Products:** 29 total. Price range $95-$599. 6+ sold out.
**Platform:** Shopify with Visa/MC/PayPal/Apple Pay/Google Pay checkout.

**Top 5 revenue leaks found:**
1. Sold-out products featured on homepage above the fold
2. $0.00 pricing on multiple products (looks like a broken site)
3. Internal product codes used as customer-facing names (KIDDIEBUNK52, LYSA33-WHITE HB FB SLATS)
4. No safety certifications, materials, or dimensions on product pages
5. No back-in-stock capture on sold-out items — buyers lost permanently
